## Decisions / things worth knowing

- A product-page read needs a product page. Listings from Serper Shopping point at constructed search pages, so WI-3's read applies to cross-fed review pages and to the on-click resolve; a ratio-flagged listing that cannot be read is treated as "nothing stated".
- Old stored blobs lack `normalized`/`excluded`/`headline_basis`. Nothing recomputes them on read; a refresh does (price ≈ 1 credit).
- Cost: **0 new Serper calls.** At most 2 local page renders per price fetch.

### Findings from the real Vietti data (explain-wine, stored pages)
With the new rules, Wine Library's plain page is exact (Tier 2 — its only critic, James Suckling, is not in the trusted set), Woodland's Castiglione and JJ Buckley's Monvigliero pages are Tier 3a "different bottling", the five stored prices are ambiguous, and the prompt names Castiglione and Monvigliero. Answering Castiglione moves the wine to Tier 1 with the Decanter score and recomputes the headline from the two listings that name it.

### Decisions / worth knowing
- A different-bottling page used to be read at once; it is now read last. Side effect: when a *primary* retailer's only page is a different bottling, the extended tier may now run (more Serper) because the primary tier "found no exact score". That is the narrow-first rule, not a bug.
- "Missing" is an evidence rule from D8: a shop that drops the vineyard from its title looks like a different bottling for a wine that records one. Tier-word-only differences never do.
- `SEPARATE_CUVEE_WORDS` (vieilles, vignes, prestige, barrique) — **confirmed by the developer 2026-10-11** (spec §8 Q3). Also confirmed that a shop dropping the vineyard the wine records counts as a different bottling (Tier 1 → 3a possible). Optional "Refresh with this bottling" button: **not yet**. Downloading and reading the Bichot sheet: **no**.
- Stored listings from before this PR have no `listing_title`, so they cannot be re-scored after an answer until a price refresh.
