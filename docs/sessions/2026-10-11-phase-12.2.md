## Decisions / things worth knowing

- A product-page read needs a product page. Listings from Serper Shopping point at constructed search pages, so WI-3's read applies to cross-fed review pages and to the on-click resolve; a ratio-flagged listing that cannot be read is treated as "nothing stated".
- Old stored blobs lack `normalized`/`excluded`/`headline_basis`. Nothing recomputes them on read; a refresh does (price ≈ 1 credit).
- Cost: **0 new Serper calls.** At most 2 local page renders per price fetch.
