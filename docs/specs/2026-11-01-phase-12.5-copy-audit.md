## Worth knowing before you decide

- **Error lines** (1, 2 and the other "Could not … — is the backend running?" rows) are proposed as one pattern, "Couldn't <do it>. Is the backend running?", so web and iOS read the same.
- **Reviews' states** (the "Review lookup failed", "Looking for critic scores…", "No critic scores found yet" rows) are proposed to mirror the price block's new lines, so the two blocks speak the same way.
- **Badge explanations** (Verified / Unverified / Search only) follow the spec's seed list; the long versions would disappear entirely, since the text is already behind a tap.
- Not proposed, deliberately: field labels, button verbs, list names, tasting-note vocabulary and the WSET terms — they are already one or two words.
